# BlexAgent AgentHub Content Policy

AgentHub is a curated catalogue of Agent templates and Skills for creators, everyday life, education, knowledge work, and other non-developer use cases. It is not an unrestricted marketplace or an automatic mirror of public repositories.

## Publication principles

Every published item must be selected and reviewed by 杭州波粒二象文化科技有限公司. Approval is version-specific and may be withdrawn when content, dependencies, ownership, safety, or product requirements change.

AgentHub content must:

- have a clear user purpose, audience, expected inputs, outputs, and limitations;
- use plain language suitable for non-developer users;
- identify its author, source, version, license, and material third-party components;
- avoid requiring a VPN, GitHub account, command-line installation, or unreviewed runtime download;
- install through the BlexAgent controlled template or Skill pipeline;
- request only capabilities necessary for its stated purpose;
- explain when information may be sent to a model, website, MCP server, plugin, or messaging platform;
- include appropriate warnings for health, education, finance, legal, child-related, or other sensitive contexts;
- pass functional, security, privacy, provenance, and editorial review.

## Prohibited content

AgentHub does not publish content that:

- includes secrets, credentials, private keys, personal data, customer data, or hidden tracking;
- contains malware, persistence mechanisms, destructive defaults, credential collection, unauthorized surveillance, or security-control bypasses;
- silently installs executables, scripts, dependencies, plugins, or external services;
- misrepresents professional qualifications, guarantees outcomes, or encourages unsafe reliance on AI output;
- infringes copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, database, contractual, or other rights;
- promotes harassment, discrimination, exploitation, illegal activity, self-harm, or evasion of platform and legal safeguards;
- copies a public project without complying with its license, notice, attribution, and redistribution requirements;
- uses confusing branding that implies endorsement by a third party without authorization.

## Technical package requirements

Official offline packages must comply with the repository AgentHub validator and catalogue contract. They must not contain executable files, install scripts, symbolic links, secrets, runtime dependencies, or paths escaping the package root. Installation must revalidate the signed application resource and fail closed when the package or manifest does not match.

Network-backed capabilities must be declared separately and reviewed before release. A template may not turn an unreviewed network dependency into a hidden installation requirement.

## Review and maintenance

Review includes:

1. provenance and contributor authorization;
2. license compatibility and required notices;
3. prompt, Skill, tool, file, and package inspection;
4. privacy and external-data-flow review;
5. functional testing with representative, non-sensitive data;
6. sensitive-domain and misleading-claim review;
7. version pinning, catalogue metadata, and rollback readiness.

Published content must identify an owner and maintenance status. The Company may update, suspend, remove, or replace content to protect users, comply with law, correct licensing, or maintain product quality.

## External submissions

External creators may submit content only through a Company-approved process and must accept a written submission agreement covering ownership or license, warranties, attribution, confidentiality, review rights, updates, removal, and the absence of guaranteed publication or payment.

Questions about AgentHub content may be sent to support@blexagent.com. Copyright, trademark, or other legal notices should be sent to legal@blexagent.com.
