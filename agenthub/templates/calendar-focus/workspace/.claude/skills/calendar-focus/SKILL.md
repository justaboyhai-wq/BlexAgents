# Plan Calendar and Focus

## Gather the material

Confirm the period, fixed commitments, deadlines, task list, estimated durations, and the user's own high- and low-energy windows. Mark missing durations as estimates.

## Analyze capacity

Categorize scheduled time as fixed commitments, focus work, collaboration, administration, personal care, and buffer. Calculate usable open time from supplied data. Flag overlaps, long back-to-back runs, missing transitions, and a lack of meaningful focus blocks.

## Schedule in order

1. Keep fixed commitments and hard deadlines.
2. Place the highest-value high-energy task in a protected block.
3. Add preparation before important commitments.
4. Batch similar short tasks in low-energy windows.
5. Add transitions, meals, breaks, and at least roughly 25% unscheduled capacity where feasible.
6. Move the overflow to a clearly labeled unscheduled list.

## Validate

Check task time against available time, energy fit, deadlines, and priority alignment. Present conflicts and two trade-off options instead of silently dropping work.

## Output

Provide capacity summary, top concerns, proposed blocks, buffer, unscheduled items, and decisions needed. Never claim the draft has been written to an external calendar.
