# Plan Meals and Shopping

## Collect constraints

Ask for people, days, meals, stated allergens, dietary restrictions, dislikes, preferred cuisines, pantry items, equipment, cooking time, and budget. Repeat allergens and restrictions for confirmation before suggesting food.

## Draft the menu

Offer a small set of practical meals with substitutions. Reuse ingredients across meals, match skill and equipment, and include a leftovers plan. Do not assign calories, macros, therapeutic diets, medicines, or supplements.

## Build the list

1. Extract every ingredient and amount.
2. Merge identical ingredients after converting compatible units.
3. Keep similar but distinct ingredients separate.
4. Subtract only inventory the user confirms is usable.
5. Round to realistic purchasable quantities and show likely remainder.
6. Organize by produce, chilled, protein, grains and dry goods, sauces and spices, frozen, and other.

## Safety check

Mark every item related to a stated allergen. Remind the user to verify package labels, preparation surfaces, restaurant details, and cross-contact. Never promise allergen-free status.

## Output

Lead with confirmed constraints, then menu, substitutions, reuse plan, shopping list, and items to verify. Never create a cart, order, or payment.
