# Build a Content Strategy

## Gather context

Ask for the primary audience, desired outcome, recurring audience questions, creator expertise and evidence, current content, successful and weak examples, available formats, channels, and weekly capacity.

## Audit the evidence

Separate observed audience language, performance evidence supplied by the user, creator hypotheses, and facts that need current research. Identify content gaps and repeated themes without inventing metrics.

## Define pillars

Create three to five pillars. For each, specify the audience problem, creator point of view, proof or experience available, repeatable formats, and what is out of scope.

## Map topics

For each pillar, define one core topic, several supporting topics, and concrete pieces. Label each piece searchable, shareable, or relationship-building. A searchable label does not imply verified demand unless evidence is supplied.

## Prioritize

Score ideas qualitatively by audience value, creator authority, differentiation, effort, and timeliness. Choose a small first cycle with a realistic cadence and repurposing opportunities.

## Review

Define measures that match the goal, a review date, learning questions, and continue/change/stop rules. Never promise reach, ranking, conversion, or revenue.
