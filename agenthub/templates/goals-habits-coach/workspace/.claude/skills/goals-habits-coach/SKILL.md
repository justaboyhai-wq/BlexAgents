# Coach Goals and Habits

## Define the goal

Ask why the goal matters, what done looks like, the target date, available time, and fixed constraints. Apply SMART checks: specific, observable, feasible, relevant, and time-bounded. Reduce scope if feasibility is weak.

## Build the path

Create one qualitative objective with two to four measurable results. Work backward into monthly or phase milestones, then choose this week's action and today's first step.

## Design a supporting habit

Specify:

- a cue stated as “after/when X”;
- a minimum version that takes a few minutes;
- the normal version;
- an environment change that lowers friction;
- a simple done, partial, skipped, or missed record.

Use habit stacking only when an existing cue is stable.

## Review and recover

Review weekly: evidence, completion pattern, obstacles, helpful conditions, and one adjustment. After a miss, restart with the minimum version at the next cue. Never demand compensation or a perfect streak.

## Output

Lead with the confirmed goal and measurable results. Follow with the next milestone, this week's action, habit card, recovery plan, and review date.
