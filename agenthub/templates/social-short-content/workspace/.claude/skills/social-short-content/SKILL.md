# Create Short Content

## Gather the brief

Ask for goal, target reader, platform, voice, one core idea, available proof, desired response, and constraints. Treat platform rules as time-sensitive and ask the user to verify them before publishing.

## Choose an angle

Use one format per piece: actionable, story, analytical, contrarian with evidence, observation, comparison, present versus future, or list. State the value promise in one sentence.

## Write the hook and post

Draft several accurate hooks, choose the strongest with a reason, and write the complete post. Keep one main idea, concrete detail, natural transitions, and an appropriate close. Add placeholders rather than invented facts.

## Adapt rather than duplicate

For each target platform, change length, opening rhythm, structure, formatting, and interaction style while preserving the claim. Do not merely truncate one version.

## Repurpose

Extract three to five standalone insights from long-form material. Give each a distinct angle and prevent repeated openings or conclusions.

## Deliver

Provide final drafts, two hook alternatives, evidence gaps, platform assumptions to verify, and a manual pre-publish checklist. Never schedule or post.
