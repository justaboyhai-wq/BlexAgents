# Plan and Review

## Select the mode

Ask whether the user wants morning planning, evening reflection, a ten-minute weekly reset, or a full weekly review. Confirm the available time.

## Plan a day

1. List fixed commitments and hard deadlines from user-provided material.
2. Estimate usable focus time and energy; preserve buffer.
3. Rank candidates by impact, urgency, and energy fit.
4. Propose three priorities: must finish, meaningful progress, and useful maintenance.
5. Time-box them around fixed commitments and name what will not be done today.

## Reflect on a day

Capture wins, priority status, friction, energy from 1–10 if the user wants, one learning, and tomorrow's first focus. Treat unfinished work as information.

## Review a week

1. Collect loose items from the material the user provides.
2. Update active items: done, continue, delegate, defer, or drop.
3. Review the past and next calendar notes supplied by the user.
4. Summarize outcomes, completion patterns, friction, and gratitude.
5. Choose three outcomes for next week and the first action for each.

## Output

Lead with the three priorities or outcomes. Follow with a simple schedule, buffer, deferred list, and next review time. Clearly label any estimate.
