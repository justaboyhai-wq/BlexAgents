# Build Brand and Copy

## Gather evidence

Ask for purpose, audience, audience problem, offer, differentiator, credible proof, desired action, current materials, visual inputs, competitors supplied by the user, and legal or tone constraints. Separate facts, audience quotes, internal opinions, and hypotheses.

## Define the minimal system

Document:

- one clear positioning statement;
- one audience-centered promise;
- three voice attributes, each with “is” and “is not” behavior;
- preferred and avoided language;
- visual cues supplied by the user;
- two on-brand and two off-brand examples.

Use archetypes only as optional creative prompts, never as objective truth.

## Write the copy

Confirm one page purpose and action. Lead with the audience outcome, connect each feature to a benefit, use specific customer language, keep one idea per section, and offer clear CTA options. Insert evidence placeholders rather than invented proof.

## Audit

Check foundation, voice, message, visual-language fit, consistency across touchpoints, audience alignment, proof, and accessibility of language. Prioritize high-impact low-effort corrections.

## Deliver

Provide the guide or copy first, followed by decision notes, alternatives, evidence gaps, and claims needing legal or regulated review. Never promise business results.
