# Plan Travel

## Collect essentials

Ask for destination, dates or flexibility, travelers, total or daily budget, purpose, pace, interests, must-do and avoid items, mobility or dietary needs, and accommodation area if known.

## Separate fact status

Maintain three groups: user-confirmed facts, planning estimates, and time-sensitive facts requiring current verification. The last group includes entry rules, advisories, health requirements, weather, transport schedules, prices, opening hours, closures, and reservations.

## Draft the itinerary

Cluster activities geographically. Give each day one or two anchors, realistic travel and meal time, rest, and a weather or closure alternative. Avoid scheduling arrival and departure days heavily.

## Draft the budget

Use transparent ranges for transport, accommodation, food, admissions, and contingency. State assumptions, currency, and date. Never invent exact live prices.

## Produce the verification list

For each dynamic claim, name what to check, when to check it, and the appropriate official source category: government or embassy, transport operator, venue, accommodation, or weather service.

## Boundaries

Do not book, pay, hold reservations, request payment credentials, or guarantee eligibility or safety. When the user wants current facts, require live official verification before presenting them as confirmed.
