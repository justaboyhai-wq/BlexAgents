# BlexAgent Team and Authorized Collaborator Conduct

This policy applies to employees, contractors, invited reviewers, content partners, and other people participating in BlexAgent repository work, release systems, support channels, meetings, messaging spaces, AgentHub review, and Company-operated workspaces.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect and evaluate work on its technical, product, safety, and user impact.
- Communicate decisions and material objections clearly and professionally.
- Protect user data, Company confidential information, credentials, signing material, and unreleased product information.
- Use access only for approved work and report accidental access or disclosure promptly.
- Raise safety, security, privacy, accessibility, legal, and quality concerns without retaliation.
- Disclose material conflicts of interest before reviewing, approving, or investigating related work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, retaliation, deliberate humiliation, unauthorized surveillance, misuse of access, disclosure of confidential information, and deliberate security-policy violations are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Report security vulnerabilities through `security@blexagent.com`; use `legal@blexagent.com` for legal or intellectual-property concerns. Other conduct concerns may be reported privately to `team@blexagent.com`.

If the person who normally receives a report is involved in the concern or has a conflict of interest, send the report to `legal@blexagent.com` and state that an independent reviewer is required. Reports should include enough factual detail for a fair assessment while avoiding unnecessary personal data, secrets, or public disclosure.

Good-faith reports and participation in an investigation must not result in retaliation.

## Response and enforcement

The Company will limit report access to people needed to assess and respond to it. A serious security, confidentiality, safety, or conduct concern may require temporary access suspension while facts are reviewed. Responses may include guidance, required training, removal from a review, restriction or revocation of access, termination of a contract or working relationship, or referral to an appropriate authority.

The reviewer must disclose conflicts, document material findings, give affected people a reasonable opportunity to provide relevant information when safe and lawful, and preserve confidentiality to the extent practical. An appeal may be sent to `legal@blexagent.com` when the original decision maker has not already acted as the independent reviewer.

Applicable employment, contractor, confidentiality, security, and legal agreements remain controlling.
