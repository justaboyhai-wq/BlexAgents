# BlexAgent Community Code of Conduct

This policy applies to participation in the BlexAgent repository, issues, pull
requests, reviews, discussions, support spaces, AgentHub submissions, events,
and other project-operated community spaces.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- treating people with respect and evaluating work on its product, technical,
  safety, accessibility, and user impact;
- communicating decisions, limitations, and material objections clearly;
- welcoming people with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- accepting constructive feedback and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- protecting personal data, credentials, signing material, and unpublished
  vulnerability details;
- disclosing material conflicts of interest before reviewing related work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
intimidation, retaliation, deliberate humiliation, sexualized attention,
doxxing, unauthorized surveillance, misuse of access, disclosure of private
information, and deliberate security-policy violations.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `team@blexagent.com`. Send security
vulnerabilities to `security@blexagent.com` and legal or intellectual-property
concerns to `legal@blexagent.com`. If the normal recipient is involved, use
`legal@blexagent.com` and request an independent reviewer.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, temporarily suspend access,
or permanently exclude a participant when necessary to protect the community.
They will limit report access to people needed to assess it, disclose conflicts,
document material findings, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reports.

## Scope

This policy governs project community behavior. It does not replace applicable
law, platform terms, employment obligations, or separate agreements. Conduct
outside project spaces may be considered when it creates a credible safety risk
for project participants.
