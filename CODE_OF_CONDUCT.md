# BlexAgent Internal Team Conduct

Everyone with access to BlexAgent systems must maintain a safe, respectful, and
professional working environment. Harassment, discrimination, threats, misuse
of access, disclosure of confidential information, retaliation, and deliberate
security-policy violations are not acceptable.

Use respectful language, review work on its technical merits, document material
decisions, protect user data, and raise safety or compliance concerns promptly.
Reports may be sent privately to team@blexagent.com and should include enough
detail for a fair investigation. Access may be suspended while a serious
security, confidentiality, or conduct concern is investigated.

This policy applies to repository work, release systems, support channels,
meetings, and other BlexAgent workspaces. Applicable employment, contractor,
security, and legal policies remain controlling.
