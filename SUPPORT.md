# BlexAgent Support

BlexAgent is developed by 杭州波粒二象文化科技有限公司. This document separates product support from security, privacy, and legal requests so sensitive reports reach the correct channel.

## Product support

For installation, startup, update, configuration, model-provider, AgentHub, Skill, task, Feishu, DingTalk, and general usage questions, email **support@blexagent.com**.

Include only the information needed to reproduce the problem:

- BlexAgent version and whether the build is preview or generally available;
- Windows or macOS version and CPU architecture;
- where the installer was obtained;
- the user-visible error and steps that triggered it;
- redacted screenshots or the smallest relevant log excerpt.

Do not send API keys, passwords, signing keys, complete conversations, complete workspaces, customer data, or unredacted logs. Replace sensitive values with placeholders.

## Other contacts

- Security vulnerability: **security@blexagent.com** and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
- Privacy request: **privacy@blexagent.com** and [Privacy Notice](specs/legal/PRIVACY.md)
- License, trademark, or legal issue: **legal@blexagent.com**
- Authorized internal development: **team@blexagent.com**

Do not submit security vulnerabilities or sensitive information through public issues. The source repository is private and its issue tracker is not the end-user support channel.

## Preview builds

The `0.7.x` line is currently a preview line. Preview builds may change, be replaced, or require a clean reinstall. Unsigned, ad-hoc-signed, unnotarized, community, and internal artifacts are test builds and are not supported official releases.

## Self-service checks

Before contacting support:

1. Confirm the installer came from an official BlexAgent location.
2. Confirm the operating system and architecture are supported by that release.
3. Restart the application and verify that the main window renders instead of remaining blank.
4. Check whether the selected model provider and connected Channel are available.
5. Revoke and replace a credential immediately if it may have been exposed.

Support responses do not replace the terms of the applicable End User License Agreement or mandatory consumer rights.
