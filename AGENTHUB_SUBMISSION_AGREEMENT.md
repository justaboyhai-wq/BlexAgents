# AgentHub Submission Agreement Requirements

This document is an internal checklist for the written agreement required before publishing externally submitted AgentHub content. It is not a click-through agreement and does not itself accept submissions.

The approved agreement template must identify 杭州波粒二象文化科技有限公司 and the contributor, and must be reviewed for the applicable submission and jurisdiction.

At minimum, the agreement should cover:

- an exact description and version of the submitted Agent template, Skill, prompt, documentation, images, examples, and related assets;
- whether ownership is assigned to the Company or the contributor grants a sufficiently broad, worldwide, transferable, sublicensable, commercial license to review, modify, combine, reproduce, distribute, market, remove, and maintain the submission;
- the contributor's authority to submit every component and all required third-party licenses, notices, permissions, and model or dataset terms;
- disclosure of AI-generated or AI-assisted material and the contributor's responsibility for human review and provenance;
- warranties that the submission contains no secrets, personal data, malware, hidden tracking, unauthorized code, or material that infringes another person's rights;
- permission for security, privacy, license, quality, and functional review;
- the Company's right to reject, edit, suspend, update, or remove the submission without a publication guarantee;
- attribution, branding, publicity, payment, tax, support, and maintenance expectations;
- confidentiality obligations for unreleased product information and review materials;
- vulnerability, takedown, infringement, and incident cooperation;
- termination, survival, governing law, dispute resolution, and required signatures.

Do not publish an external submission based only on an email, public repository URL, open-source label, or verbal permission. Preserve the executed agreement and the reviewed source package with the catalogue release record.
